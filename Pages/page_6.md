# ASIC Tutor

A rustlings-style exercise runner for HDL: small self-checking problems you fix and re-run, graded locally in seconds. Built to sit under [ASIC 101](page_5.md) as the drill ground — reps on one concept at a time, rather than one long project.

**Stage:** in development. **The repository is private.**

## Access

Ask a club officer for access. Contact `umnasicclub@gmail.com`.

## What is documented here

Nothing further, deliberately. While the repo is private, its layout, exercise set, and open issues are not described on this public site.

Once it is made public, this page should be filled out like every other project page — what it is, what stage it is at, what is in the repo, where to start, and what is missing. See [contributing](page_17.md).

[The curriculum track](page_4.md) explains the intended split: Tutor for fast reps, ASIC 101 for the full flow on one design.
